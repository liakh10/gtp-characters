GTP Characters

Eight playable legends, one city, nobody is the good one A character switch wheel and nothing to press

01 · OverviewWhat is GTP: Characters

Grand Theft Pons had the cover, the map and the delay. This is the cast page: the eight faces that have been in every timeline since 2010, finally living in the same city on the same coast at the same sunset.

Scroll and the game switches character. The screen blows out, the wheel turns, the new one is standing there with their own stats already filled in. It is the switch from the game, without the game.

Format
Eight full screen key arts, one switch wheel, four stat bars
Palette
Hot pink, amber, teal, film grain, sun on the water
Sister site
Grand Theft Pons, the cover and the countdown that keeps slipping
Chain
Robinhood Chain, token through the Pons launchpad

02 · RosterThe eight

playable ├── Doge the driver has never once explained where he is going ├── Pepe the dealer sells nothing, knows everybody, never leaves the pool ├── Wojak the night shift drives until six in the morning, then thinks about it ├── Trollface the hacker was in the room the whole time and said nothing ├── Gigachad the door decides who gets in, and it is not you ├── Cheems the badge writes all of it down and files none of it ├── Grumpy Cat the boss says no first, then asks what it was └── Pink Wojak the mark bought the top, held the bag, is somehow still here

They are types, not people. Nobody real is being cast here and none of them are based on anyone living.

03 · StatsFour bars

Driving, Shooting, Stamina, Luck. They refill on every switch and none of them are balanced. The boss cannot drive. The mark has ninety six stamina and four luck, which is the entire joke and also the whole story.

Highest driving
Doge, ninety eight, and he still will not say where
Highest luck
Grumpy Cat, one hundred, because she stopped taking risks
Lowest luck
Pink Wojak, four, and he has never once complained

04 · ControlsUsing the site

Scroll
Switches character. One per screen, eight in total
The wheel
Bottom right. It turns so the active face is always at the top
Sound
Quiet until you tap. Your first click anywhere starts a slow loop
The end
The full cast in a grid, then the contract on a loading slab

06 · PrivacyNothing is stored

No backend, no account, no cookies, nothing written to your browser. Eight pictures, eight faces and your scroll position.

Music
One CC0 loop from OpenGameArt, plays after your first tap
Reduced motion
If your system asks for it the grain and the switch flash stop, the characters still change
Affiliation
None. Not a game, not connected to any game or studio, not advice
